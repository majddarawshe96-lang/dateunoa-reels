# dateunoa: Privacy Policy for the comment reply service

Last updated: October 26, 2026

dateunoa ("we") runs a small service that sends an automatic private message when someone comments a keyword on one of our Instagram or Facebook posts.

## What we receive
When you comment on our posts, Meta sends us the comment text, the comment ID, and your account's app-scoped ID and username.

## How we use it
We use it only to:
- send you one private message with the content you requested by commenting the keyword;
- post a short public reply under your comment saying the message was sent.

We do not sell, share or use this information for advertising, and we do not build profiles.

## What we keep
We keep only the IDs of comments we have already answered, so we never message you twice for the same comment. We keep at most the last 5,000 IDs and delete older ones automatically. We do not store comment text, usernames or message content.

## Your choices
- You can ignore or delete the message at any time.
- If you don't want to receive messages, don't comment the keyword.
- To ask us to delete any data related to you, message @dateunoa on Instagram or write to majd.darawshe.96@gmail.com, and we will delete it within 30 days.
